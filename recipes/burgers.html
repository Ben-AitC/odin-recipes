Best Burger Recipe


Ingredients

  • 1 lb 80/20
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper

Preparation

  1. Mix ingredients gently by hand.
  2. Avoid overworking the meat.

Shaping

Form into 1/2-inch thick patties.
Press a small indentation in the center with your thumb;
this prevents the burger from puffing up into a ball while cooking.

Finally the Good Part : Cooking

Grill or pan-fry on medium-high heat for 3–5 minutes per side.
For a cheeseburger, add a slice of American or Cheddar during the last minute of cooking
